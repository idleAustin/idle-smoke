# Idle Smoke Break

A satirical competitive smoke-break simulator where employees mash drags during a timed break to climb the socioeconomic ladder from back-alley service grit to white-collar luxury.

## Language

### Progression & Modes

**Mode**:
A top-level way to play selected from the pre-shift briefing deck (`Campaign`, `Head-to-Head`, or `Sandbox`).
_Avoid_: Phase, level, tier

**Phase**:
One of the three socioeconomic chapters of the Campaign journey (`Phase 1: Started from the Bottom`, `Phase 2: Middle Class`, `Phase 3: White-Collar`), each containing four sectors and culminating in a Promotion.
_Avoid_: Mode, world, act, tier

**Sector**:
An individual smoke-break environment on a Phase's facility map where a player clocks in for a shift and accumulates location-specific smoked butts.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, map, piece

**Promotion**:
The three-round qualifying gauntlet unlocked after clearing all four sectors in a Phase, requiring the player to pass three escalating 15-second shifts (`Round 1`, `Round 2`, `Round 3`) to advance to the next Phase.
_Avoid_: Boss mode, boss fight, boss level

**Promotion Round**:
One of the three sequential 15-second shifts within a Promotion, each with a higher single-shift cigarette target than the last.
_Avoid_: Stage, wave, heat

### Shift Gameplay

**Shift**:
A single 15-second smoke-break run from the first drag until the break clock expires.
_Avoid_: Match, game, session

**Drag**:
A single player input that advances the active cigarette toward completion.
_Avoid_: Press, tap, click, puff

**Butt**:
A fully smoked cigarette completed during a shift.
_Avoid_: Point, score, smoke

**Drag Ratio**:
The number of drags required to finish one cigarette at the player's current momentum or conditioning tier.
_Avoid_: Tap ratio, presses per cigarette, combo multiplier

**Callsign**:
A player's uppercase display identifier on shift reports and leaderboards.
_Avoid_: Username, handle, nickname
